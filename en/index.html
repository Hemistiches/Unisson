The name

In unison, each voice keeps its own timbre, and all play the same note.

Laboratories, healthcare professionals, hospitals, learned societies and patient associations each have their own role, and each remains independent. What they share is one score: the laws and regulations that govern their interactions.

Within the company, the same rule applies to every team: medical affairs, marketing, events, legal. And in the paperwork, agreements, payments and disclosures must all tell the same story.

What Unisson covers

Two separate French regimes govern these interactions. The first, known as the "anti-gift" rules, prohibits benefits offered to healthcare professionals, save for exceptions subject to declaration or authorisation. The second, known as "transparency", makes agreements, remuneration and benefits public. One operation may fall under both, or only one: everything starts with qualifying it.

  • Experts and servicesAdvice, expertise, speaking engagements: a genuine need, and remuneration proportionate to the service rendered (art. L. 1453-7 of the French Public Health Code).
  • Scientific committeesAdvisory boards and expert groups: selecting participants, agreements, justifying the amounts.
  • Congresses and hospitalityHospitality of a reasonable level, limited to the purpose of the event and to the professionals concerned.
  • Donations, grants, partnershipsHospitals, learned societies, patient associations: these recipients are not all subject to the same rules.
  • Benefits frameworkThe prohibition in principle and its exceptions, with their formalities (art. L. 1453-3 and L. 1453-7).
  • Transparency of interestsPublication of agreements, remuneration and benefits on a single public website (art. L. 1453-1).
  • Approval workflowWho qualifies, who approves, who tracks the formalities and their deadlines.
  • TraceabilityBeing able to reconstruct each collaboration: agreement, approval, proof of performance, disclosures.
  • Integrity around these interactionsGifts and hospitality to other contacts, intermediaries, conflicts of interest, whistleblowing procedure.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to start with a diagnostic?

No. A collaboration or an operation can be secured on its own, and ongoing support can start directly if your practices already hold. The diagnostic is for when you do not know what your practices are worth; if it leads to the base, it is credited against it.

Do we need software?

No. Software helps record and disclose; it does not tell you what must be disclosed, on what basis, or under which agreement. I work with the tool or spreadsheets you already use, and the base provides a ready-to-use tracking table.

Our group already has procedures. What is left to do?

Checking them against French rules. Procedures designed for several countries rarely address the formalities specific to France. The diagnostic compares them with your local practices and says what needs adapting.

Unisson or Cadence?

Unisson is a targeted engagement: securing your collaborations and leaving you tools your teams use on their own. Cadence provides your legal function over time, compliance included. Setting things up falls under Unisson; day-to-day operation can fit within the Cadence volume.

Is clinical research covered?

For the relationship with investigators and the related benefits. The agreement with the hospital, the protocol, filings with the authorities and the rest of the trial framework are quoted separately; data protection for the study falls under Diapason.

Are medical device companies concerned?

Yes. These rules apply to companies that manufacture or market health products, medical devices included. Unisson is aimed first at pharmaceutical companies, but applies to any healthcare company. Device-specific regulation falls under Concordance.

Does the test result commit me?

No. It is an indicative orientation, based on your answers alone: neither legal advice, nor a contractual offer, nor a compliance certification. Scope and price are confirmed in a conversation, then in a fee agreement.
